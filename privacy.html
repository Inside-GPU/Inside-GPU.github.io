Privacy Policy

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Inside-GPU handles information when you visit our website or contact us regarding our Unity assets. We are committed to maintaining the privacy and security of our users' information.

Information Collection

Inside-GPU does not use tracking cookies or collect personal data from visitors who simply browse this website. The only personal information we receive is what you voluntarily provide when you contact us via email (such as your name and email address).

Use of Information

Any information provided through email correspondence is used strictly for technical support, business inquiries, or providing feedback. We do not sell, trade, or share your contact information with third parties.

Third-Party Services

Our website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect some server logs and basic telemetry as part of their hosting service. Please refer to GitHub's privacy policy for more details regarding their data handling.

Contact

If you have any questions regarding this policy, please reach out to us at info.insidegpu@gmail.com.
